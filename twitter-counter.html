Twitter/X limits to know

280 characters per tweet — that includes spaces, emojis and links. Each link counts as 23 characters regardless of its actual length.

Threads let you tell longer stories — just number each tweet (1/5, 2/5, etc.) so readers know the order.

Pinned tweets are your profile's first impression — keep them short, punchy and with a clear call to action.

Counter FAQ

Do emojis count as one character?

Most emojis count as 2 characters in Twitter's system, so a single emoji may reduce your limit by 2 instead of 1.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Counting happens locally in your browser as you type; nothing leaves your device.
