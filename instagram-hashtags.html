How to use hashtags on Instagram

Mix sizes. Combine a few huge hashtags (#travel, millions of posts) with medium and niche ones (#solotravelportugal, thousands). Niche hashtags give you better reach per post.

3–15 is the sweet spot. Instagram allows up to 30, but most creators see better results with 8–15 highly relevant hashtags than spamming 30 random ones.

Put them in the first comment. Keeps your caption clean while still being discoverable.

Hashtag FAQ

Do banned hashtags hurt my reach?

Yes. If a hashtag is banned or restricted, Instagram may limit your post's reach. Avoid overly generic spammy tags like #likeforlike or #followme.

Can I reuse the same hashtags every post?

Instagram may flag repetitive identical sets as spam. Rotate your hashtags and create different sets for different types of content.
