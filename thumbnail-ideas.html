What makes a thumbnail work

One clear hook. The viewer should understand the video in 1 second. One big emotion, one big statement, one face.

High contrast. Bright subject against a darker background. Yellow, red and orange pop most on YouTube's dark and light themes.

Few words. 2–4 words max in the thumbnail. The title carries the rest.

Thumbnail FAQ

Should I put my face in every thumbnail?

Not every video, but consistent branding helps. If you show your face, use an exaggerated expression — surprise, shock, excitement — that matches the title.

Does the thumbnail affect CTR?

Yes — it's half of the click decision (the other half is the title). A boring thumbnail kills even a great title.
