What makes a good YouTube title?

Search + curiosity. The best titles tell viewers exactly what the video is about while leaving just enough mystery to click. Include your main keyword early.

Numbers work. "7 Ways to Save Money" performs better than "Ways to Save Money" — numbers set expectations and feel scannable.

Keep it under 60 characters. YouTube cuts off long titles in search results and on mobile. Front-load the important words.

Title FAQ

Should I use clickbait?

Use curiosity, not deception. Overpromising and underdelivering hurts your watch time and tells YouTube your video isn't satisfying — which hurts future recommendations.

Does the title affect SEO?

Yes. YouTube uses the title (along with tags and description) to understand your video and show it in search and suggested videos.

Does it work offline?

Yes — title generation happens entirely in your browser.
