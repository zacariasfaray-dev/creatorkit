What the number means

1–3%: Average. Most creators sit here — normal for accounts with a few thousand followers.

3–6%: Good. Your audience is engaged and responsive to your content.

6%+: Excellent. Strong community loyalty — this is what brands look for in collaborations.

Under 1%: Low. Consider posting at better times, improving hooks, or engaging more with comments.

Engagement FAQ

Which platforms does this work for?

Instagram, TikTok and YouTube all use likes + comments ÷ followers for engagement rate. The formula is the same; only your audience behavior differs.

Should I use total likes or per-post likes?

Use a single post's likes and comments divided by your follower count at that time. For an account average, average the last 10–15 posts first.

Is my data uploaded?

No — the calculation runs locally in your browser.
